## Code review

Normal review reports up to 3 findings; deep review up to 5. Additional P0/P1 findings may exceed either cap. Report the absence of actionable findings when the evidence supports that conclusion.

Rank findings before applying the cap. Give severity, category, changed location, evidence, impact, and a practical improvement with its tradeoff. Describe estimated value proportionally and keep the result concise.
